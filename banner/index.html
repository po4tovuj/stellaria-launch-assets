Important Safety Information

⚠ WARNING: ION DRIVE CO-EXPOSURE AND CARDIAC ARRHYTHMIA

Concurrent ion drive co-exposure within 6 hours of Stellaria administration has been associated with prolonged QT interval and, in rare cases, Torsades-class arrhythmia during cryo-induction. Do not administer Stellaria within 6 hours of active ion drive proximity (≤40 m, shielded), or within 12 hours (unshielded). Obtain a baseline ECG prior to first dose and prior to any subsequent cryo-induction cycle.

Indication. STELLARIA (morphanidine-C) tablets are indicated as hibernation-assist therapy in licensed deep-space personnel ≥21 years of age undergoing planned cryo-induction on missions of 90 days or longer.

Contraindications. Known hypersensitivity to morphanidine-C; active cryo-sensitization syndrome (CSS); uncorrected Grade II telomeric instability; Class-III temporal stabilizers; pregnancy.

Warnings and Precautions. See Boxed Warning. Hypothalamic set-point drift; monitor core temperature 90 min post-thaw. No EVA duties for 120 min post-thaw.

Adverse Reactions. Most common (≥5%): dry mouth (11%), mild paresthesia (9%), transient temporal displacement (7%), metallic taste (6%), mild photosensitivity (5%).

Drug Interactions. Avoid Class-III temporal stabilizers and MAOIs within 14 days. Caffeine analogs >400 mg/day may blunt induction onset.

Use in Specific Populations. Pregnancy: contraindicated. Lactation: not recommended. Pediatric (<21 y): not established. Hepatic/renal impairment: reduce dose 50% moderate; contraindicated severe.
